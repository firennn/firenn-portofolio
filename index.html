NLP · OCR · Team Research

AllergenScan

An end-to-end pipeline that reads real Indonesian food packaging photos and flags allergens. It combines PP-OCRv5 OCR with a fine-tuned IndoBERT NER model, trained via a distant-supervision pipeline built on a BPOM/EU-FIR aligned allergen dictionary so that the model understands context instead of just normal string matching.
